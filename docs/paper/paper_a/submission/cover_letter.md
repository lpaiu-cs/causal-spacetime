Dear Editors of *Classical and Quantum Gravity*,

Please consider my manuscript, “Spacetime quantities from causal order: an
operational ladder with preregistered validations at finite density,” for
publication in *Classical and Quantum Gravity* as a Research Paper.

Causal structure determines conformal geometry, while volume information
fixes the remaining scale under standard hypotheses. The manuscript asks the
corresponding finite-data question: given events and their causal order, which
spacetime quantities can an explicit procedure reconstruct, what additional
information does each reconstruction require, and which ambiguities remain
when that information is absent?

The central contribution is a branched dependency ledger connecting causal
order to dimension estimates, proper-time calibration, radar coordinates,
oriented Lorentz maps, observer atlases, and measure-dependent volume
reconstruction. Each step names its supplied ingredient and reports its
finite-density behaviour. Three counterexamples set the boundary of the
ledger: order alone supplies no metric scale, a single observer leaves a
reflection ambiguity, and finite signal speed alone does not imply Lorentzian
geometry. A Rindler analogue gives the expected accessibility boundary.

The manuscript also tests the framework in curved spacetime using
preregistered numerical stages. In a vacuum plane wave whose coordinate volume form and Poisson
sprinkling law are exactly those of the flat comparator, an order-only
statistic detects a deliberately strong pure-Weyl deformation at finite
density. Separate stages on a frozen Schwarzschild exterior patch confirm the
paired causal-relation shift and detect single-poset discrimination with
incomplete separation. Finally, a four-rung mass ladder compares sprinkled
counts with independently certified continuum four-volumes under a frozen
2.5% equivalence rule. These results are presented as controlled validations
of reconstruction procedures at specified operating points, not as evidence
that spacetime is discrete or reducible to causal order.

I believe the manuscript fits CQG because it connects Lorentzian causal
structure, discrete-spacetime methods, black-hole exterior numerics, and
operational measurement while placing the results in their wider
gravitational-physics context. The evidence trail is auditable: decision rules
for the curved-space stages were archived before execution, reported figures
are regenerated from version-controlled artifacts, and the manuscript states
the limitations of every primary verdict.

The manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not
under consideration elsewhere. There are no conflicts of interest. The code
and supporting data are identified in the Data Availability Statement.
