Dear Editors of *Classical and Quantum Gravity*,

Please consider my manuscript, “Spacetime quantities from causal order: an
operational ladder with preregistered validations at finite density,” for
publication in *Classical and Quantum Gravity* as a Research Paper.

Causal structure determines conformal geometry, while volume information
fixes the remaining scale under standard hypotheses. The manuscript asks the
corresponding finite-data question: which spacetime quantities can explicit
procedures reconstruct from events and causal order, what additional structure
does each require, and what remains ambiguous when that structure is absent?

The main contribution is a branched dependency ledger from causal order to
dimension estimates, proper-time calibration, radar coordinates, oriented
Lorentz maps, observer atlases, and measure-dependent volume reconstruction.
Each step declares its input and finite-density behaviour. Counterexamples mark
hard boundaries: order alone supplies no metric scale, a single observer leaves
a reflection ambiguity, and finite signal speed alone does not imply
Lorentzian geometry.

Preregistered curved-space stages test the ledger at specified operating
points. In a vacuum plane wave with the flat comparator's coordinate volume
form and sprinkling law, an order-only statistic detects a deliberately strong
pure-Weyl deformation. A frozen Schwarzschild exterior stage confirms paired
causal-relation shifts and single-poset discrimination with incomplete
separation. A four-rung mass ladder compares sprinkled counts with
independently certified continuum four-volumes under a frozen 2.5% equivalence
rule.

The manuscript fits CQG by connecting Lorentzian causal structure,
discrete-spacetime methods, black-hole exterior numerics, and operational
measurement. Its claims are deliberately conservative: these are validations
of reconstruction procedures, not evidence that spacetime is discrete or
reducible to causal order. Decision rules were archived before execution, and
reported figures are regenerated from version-controlled artifacts.

The manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not
under consideration elsewhere. There are no conflicts of interest. The code
and supporting data are identified in the Data Availability Statement.
