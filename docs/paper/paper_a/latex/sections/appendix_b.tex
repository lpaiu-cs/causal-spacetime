\section{Capstone execution provenance}\label{app:provenance}

The \sref{sec:capstone} result is reproducible not merely in the sense
that its code is committed, but in the sense that the \emph{order of
operations}---freeze before execution, execution before recording---is
provable from the repository's commit graph and enforced by mechanical
gates.

\textbf{Commit chain.} Design and probe chain: PR \#38--\#48.
Preregistration: preparation commit (PR \#49), S1 cost measurement
(PR \#50), then $P' = \texttt{51875a2}$ (a one-source seed-ledger fix)
$\to$ preflight executed on a clean checkout of $P'$, its artifact
recording \texttt{code\_version} $= P'$ and the SHA-256 digests of every
execution-relevant source file $\to$ final-freeze commit
$F = \texttt{b858b08}$ carrying the certification artifact and the freeze
manifest (preflight digest, \texttt{preflight\_code\_version} $= P'$, the
S1 price sentence, the frozen sizes and seeds; PR \#51) $\to$ the
campaign executed on a checkout of exactly $F$ (not a descendant merge
commit, so the recorded executing HEAD is $F$ itself) $\to$ results
commit $R = \texttt{5126ddf}$, a direct child of $F$ (PR \#52). A test
asserts $P' \prec F \prec R$ with
\texttt{git merge-base -{}-is-ancestor}.

\textbf{Gates.} Stochastic modes refuse a dirty worktree at entry. The
campaign mode refuses to run unless (i) the freeze manifest exists,
(ii) its recorded preflight digest matches the committed preflight
artifact, and (iii) the source digests certified at preflight entry match
the checked-out files---captured at entry and re-checked at exit, so a
mid-run edit aborts the run with nothing recorded. Interrupted runs
restart the same seed; an ambiguity violation blocks the stage rather
than permitting a seed swap.

\textbf{Artifacts.} The complete \sref{sec:capstone} evidence bundle,
with digests, is enumerated in \artifact{artifact\_\allowbreak{}manifest.md}; the
per-sentence mapping from manuscript text to evidence file is recorded
there as the citation-to-artifact inventory.

\textbf{Frozen sentences.} Each preregistered stage fixes, before
execution, the sentence its verdict would license, and the campaign
records that sentence in its results artifact. Those originals are
written in Korean, the working language of this program's records; every
frozen sentence printed in \sref{sec:preregresult} and
\sref{sec:schwarzschild} is therefore a translation, and the artifact
holds the authoritative text. What keeps the two from drifting apart is
not editorial care: a contract test reads each original from its
artifact, holds it against the rendering the manuscript prints in its
place, and fails if either side moves---so a translation cannot quietly
soften, strengthen, or outrun the sentence the campaign actually
licensed. The paper prints no Korean, and a separate test enforces that,
because a script the reader cannot check would put the load-bearing
wording beyond the reader's reach.

\textbf{Section~\ref{sec:schwarzschild} (Schwarzschild extension)
provenance.} Freeze commit \texttt{ceed85d} (PR \#57, merge
\texttt{5cffd90}) carries the frozen rule, the fresh seed registration,
the runner, the contract tests, and the 8-file content-addressed freeze
manifest---and no results. The campaign executed once on a clean checkout
of exactly \texttt{ceed85d}; the runner captured the git state at entry,
refused a dirty tree, verified every manifest digest at entry, and
re-verified both at exit before writing, so the artifact's recorded
lineage is \texttt{start == end == ceed85d}, clean,
\texttt{run\_kind = fresh\_observation}. The results commit (PR \#58,
merge \texttt{492e61f}) added the artifact, moved the campaign seed from
fresh to observed in the ledger, and flipped the runner's campaign path
to a deterministic replay whose output is owned by a separate file and
can never replace the fresh observation. Because that flip legitimately
changed two protocol-surface files, the \emph{current} tree's manifest
hashes the post-result replay surface; the manifest that governed the
executed run is preserved byte-for-byte as
\artifact{docs/\allowbreak{}prereg/\allowbreak{}p14\_\allowbreak{}s4\_\allowbreak{}executed\_\allowbreak{}freeze\_\allowbreak{}manifest.json}, and a
contract test verifies that snapshot against the historical blobs at
\texttt{ceed85d} directly. Interrupted runs restart the same seed;
undecided pairs are absorbed by identified intervals rather than a stop
rule.

\textbf{Section~\ref{sec:schwarzschild} second stage (S5) provenance.}
Freeze commit \texttt{1c26224} (PR \#60, merge \texttt{3aeb8e5}) carried
the rule, the fresh seed registrations, the runner, and the power
certification; an execution review then caught an
exact-versus-approximate chi-square mismatch in the certified resampling
source, and the corrected freeze is \texttt{86e3674} (PR \#61, merge
\texttt{f286508})---certification unchanged under the exact factor. The
campaign executed once on a clean checkout of exactly \texttt{86e3674}
(entry == exit git state, the 8-file content-addressed manifest verified
at both ends, fail-closed CLI, \texttt{run\_kind = fresh\_observation},
curved seed 40000251 / flat seed 40000261). The results commit (PR \#62,
merge \texttt{d1e0d20}) added the artifact, moved both seeds to observed,
flipped the campaign path to a replay whose output is owned by a separate
file, and preserved the manifest that governed the executed run
byte-for-byte as
\artifact{docs/\allowbreak{}prereg/\allowbreak{}p14\_\allowbreak{}s5\_\allowbreak{}executed\_\allowbreak{}freeze\_\allowbreak{}manifest.json}, verified
against the historical blobs at \texttt{86e3674} by a contract test; the
\emph{current} \artifact{p14\_\allowbreak{}s5\_\allowbreak{}freeze\_\allowbreak{}manifest.json} hashes the
post-result replay surface.

\textbf{Auxiliary O4b instrument-audit provenance (oracle arc).} The
certified oracle chain is O1 $\to$ O2 $\to$ O3 (PR \#64--\#68):
flight-time certification, certified cell integrator, then the O3 freeze
(\texttt{785148e}) and its one approved execution producing the certified
volume (\artifact{docs/\allowbreak{}prereg/\allowbreak{}p14\_\allowbreak{}o3\_\allowbreak{}volume.json}, executed surface
\artifact{p14\_\allowbreak{}o3\_\allowbreak{}executed\_\allowbreak{}freeze\_\allowbreak{}manifest.json}). The first audit
stage, O4, aborted at its G3 with no verdict (PR \#69--\#70); its replay
diagnostic and G3 redesign reopened the preregistration (PR \#71--\#74),
and O4b was frozen with new seeds (PR \#75). The approved execution SHA
is the freeze branch head \texttt{715865a}---not the merge commit. The
campaign executed once from that clean exact checkout: G3a passed (71
wrapper-contract cases, 6,537 metered calls),
\artifact{refs/\allowbreak{}o4b/\allowbreak{}reservation} was claimed (\texttt{46acee34}, retained
permanently, spending seeds 40000401/40000411), G1 ran to 26,200,000
points and G2 to 1,072,696 points, and the run stopped at the
pre-publication provenance re-read on a wiring defect---the claim
callback returned \texttt{None}---publishing nothing. The preservation
commit (PR \#76, merge \texttt{83e366c}) committed the incident and
checkpoint verbatim (\artifact{p14\_\allowbreak{}o4b\_\allowbreak{}incident.json},
\artifact{p14\_\allowbreak{}o4b\_\allowbreak{}checkpoint.json}), retired both seeds in the ledger,
and snapshotted the executed surface byte-for-byte
(\artifact{p14\_\allowbreak{}o4b\_\allowbreak{}executed\_\allowbreak{}freeze\_\allowbreak{}manifest.json}, byte-identical to
the freeze blob at \texttt{715865a}). The recovery commit (PR \#77, merge
\texttt{50586dd}) published \artifact{p14\_\allowbreak{}o4b\_\allowbreak{}results.json} with
\texttt{run\_kind = recovered\_completed\_campaign} by re-applying the
frozen decision functions to the preserved statistics---bit-identical to
the preserved verdicts, inputs authenticated by blob SHA-256 pins and an
incident/checkpoint cross-check, the remote reservation re-verified
immediately before publication; per the frozen stage-verdict table (G1
and G2 both concordant, G3a valid, frozen $n$ reached) the stage verdict
is CONCORDANT. The maintenance commit (PR \#78, merge \texttt{a04e860})
fixed the wiring defects---the claim return value, the budget stage
label, an explicit incident \texttt{failure\_point}, a named checkpoint
RNG stream---with end-to-end regression tests over the real
\texttt{main()} path, leaving the result, incident, checkpoint, and
executed snapshot untouched. No campaign rerun and no same-seed re-entry
occurred anywhere in this chain; the historical no-verdict records of the
O4 abort and the O4b incident retain their original verdicts, with the
recovery linked rather than retroactively regraded.
