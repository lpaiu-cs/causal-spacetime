## Scope and relation to Paper B

Paper A is a **branched reconstruction dependency ledger**: given a causal
(accessibility) order plus a declared, minimal ingredient set — a counting
measure, an observer chain with clock labels, an orientation reference, an
observer atlas, or local measure information — which spacetime quantities can
be operationally reconstructed, and which cannot. It re-derives standard causal-set and
radar-protocol results as controlled validations and, as its own contribution,
makes explicit the accounting of what each rung requires and the negative
results that bound it.

Paper B (separate) takes the next question — whether observer-relative distance
*order* can be validated as recovering latent geometry — builds a preregistered
discriminator for it, and carries that instrument through geometry dilution,
2+1D dimension selection, and an emergence chain (growth dynamics; an exact
obstruction to unrestricted action weighting; and the action-weighted 2D-orders
ensemble). At N=600, selected random-start post-burn-in configurations pass and
the bipartite-start beta=32 control blocks, but these are not certified
equilibrium draws and do not establish a transition or finite-size scaling.
Paper A is the foundation Paper B stands on: its verified
reconstruction layer is the measurement side of Paper B's instrument.
