Please consider my manuscript, "Detecting sub-discreteness gravitational waves from causal order alone: finite-density calibration, crossover, and phase-decoherence limits", for publication as a research article.

**Context and core advance.** Whether causal set partial orders encode physical structure at scales shorter than the mean discreteness spacing $\ell = N^{-1/4}$ is a basic open problem in discrete Lorentzian geometry. For an exact circularly polarized vacuum plane-wave family with wavelength $\lambda = N^{-1/\alpha}$, prior asymptotic theorems proved indistinguishability for all fixed $\alpha < 4/3$ and an infeasible existence separation for $\alpha > 2$ whose thresholds require uncomputable $\sim 10^{-38}$ anchor probabilities and wavelengths $\lambda \sim 10^{-40}$. Conversely, local fifth-degree polynomial Weyl jets recover all ten Weyl components cleanly when $\lambda \gtrsim T$ but collapse through oscillatory cancellation once $r = T/\lambda \gtrsim 8$.

This manuscript operationalizes the sub-discreteness band at finite $N \le 2^{23}$ without prior coordinates, probe labels, or known background focusing $q$:

1. *Phase-decoherence and conformal mechanisms:* Sparse conformal-projected constraint Hessian spectra and truth-referenced triangulations across independent $N=2^{20}$ realizations isolate why longitudinal phase coherence $|\langle e^{i(\hat{u}-u)/\lambda}\rangle|$ drops from $0.81$ ($\alpha=2.86$) to $0.05$ ($\alpha=2.00$) as triangulation error $\sigma(\hat{u})/\lambda$ exceeds unity.
2. *Blind order-only spectral detector:* Progressive $10{+}10$ order-ring calibration, $4\text{D}$ probe triangulation, Neyman $C(\alpha)$ nuisance projection against anchor and $q$ modes, chiral spin-2 matched filtering, and two-component ($c_1/\omega^2 + c_2/\omega^4$) scale-invariant Huberized Gamma-Whittle whitening eliminate zero-whitening, low-frequency null pile-up (`28/48` in initial campaign S1 to `3/56` in S2; `36/60` to `3/72` across all nulls), and calibration-trap failures.
3. *Wavelength crossover and preregistered sub-discreteness test:* Local fifth-degree Weyl jets fall from $0.749$ (95% CI $[0.720, 0.778]$) at $r=4$ to $0.0119$ ($[0.0083, 0.0154]$) at $r=32$, where spectral detection already reaches `24/24` ($q=4, r=32$) and `24/24` ($q=1, r=48$). Across `268` held-out runs up to $N = 2^{23}$ (`S2`, `20/20` preregistered gates confirmed after preserving `S1` `16/18` verbatim), pooled null false positives are `1/56` (1.8%, $[0.003, 0.094]$), primary sub-discreteness waves ($\alpha = 3.67 \to 3.29$) detect at `130/132` (98.5%; `28/28` at $r=128$, `36/36` Poisson random phase, `132/132` wavenumber lock), and blind focusing error stays $|\hat{q}-q| \le 0.0173$ (upper 95% CI) across all primary arms.

**Honest limits stated in the manuscript.** The detector assumes the circular plane-wave background geometry class for anchor triangulation and tests stationary monochromatic waves of $O(1)$ coarse-grained focusing parameter $q$; detection establishes narrowband spectral separation and background-$q$ recovery rather than full $10$-component point-tensor reconstruction, we transparently document the S1-to-S2 whitening iteration, and confirmed sub-discreteness evidence terminates within our GPU budget at $\alpha = 3.29$ ($r=128, N=2^{23}$) without claiming to break the $\alpha \approx 2$ phase-scrambling wall.
